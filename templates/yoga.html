🧘 Gentle Yoga with Marlijn

We're trying out gentle yoga on a few Fridays this summer, and hope to run it more regularly from September.

We are trialling gentle, neurodivergent-friendly yoga sessions as part of Fridays @ Farringdon.

These sessions are designed to be beginner-friendly, accessible and low-pressure. You do not need previous yoga experience. The session will be adapted as much as possible around the needs of those attending.

The sessions will take place on the outdoor terrace at Pan Macmillan, 6 Briset Street, London, EC1M 5NR. Places are limited to 8 people per session because the terrace space is small.

Please bring your own yoga mat and wear loose, comfortable clothing. As the session may take place in sunny weather, please consider wearing suncream and bringing a hat or water bottle if helpful.

🌿 The terrace

Sessions take place on our calm outdoor terrace, high above Farringdon with open views across London.

📅 Dates & booking

🕤 You're welcome to arrive from 9:30am — that's when the terrace is available — and the yoga session begins at 10:00am.

This is not a drop-in activity — please register in advance. Places are limited to 8 per session, and a session that is full can no longer be selected.

Yoga is booked in the same place as everything else at Fridays @ Farringdon. Choose your Friday, pick Gentle Yoga with Marlijn, and answer a few questions so Marlijn can adapt the session for you.

All upcoming dates — and how many places are left on each one — are shown there, so what you see is always current.

Please note: we may need to contact you regarding your booking — for example if a session is cancelled or there's another relevant update.
