Please book before you visit. Fridays @ Farringdon takes place at the offices of our host, book publisher Pan Macmillan. Their reception team needs your name on the attendee list and will have to turn you away if you arrive without a booking.

You must register for each Friday you want to attend before Thursday at 10am (London time). There is no registration on the day.

Signing up for email reminders does not book a place. You must complete the booking form and receive your booking confirmation before you come. Register for Fridays @ Farringdon.
