Room Bookings for Fridays @ Farringdon

Book each Friday before 10am on the preceding Thursday (London time). Pan Macmillan reception needs your name on the attendee list. Wait for your booking confirmation before coming. There are no bookings on the day.

Fridays @ Farringdon is a self-directed space. If you need personal care, one-to-one support or supervision, bring someone who can provide it. Our volunteers are hosts.

Before booking, read Kirsty's help sheet for access and emergencies, and our community guidelines. Links open in new tabs.
