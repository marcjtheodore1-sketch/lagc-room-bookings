ℹ️ Before you book — what to expect from this space

Fridays @ Farringdon is a relaxed, self-directed community space. Our volunteers are friendly hosts, not carers — they aren't able to provide personal care, one-to-one support, or supervision.

If you need that kind of support during the day, you will need to bring someone with you who provides it — a carer, support worker, family member or friend. Please don't attend on your own if you need that level of support: we aren't equipped for it, and it isn't safe for you.

There's a question about this when you book, so we can plan numbers and be ready for you both.
